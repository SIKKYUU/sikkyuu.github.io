About

Developing sensitive analytical systems for biosensing applications.

I am interested in analytical chemistry and bioengineering approaches that improve the sensitivity, readability, and practical utility of biosensing platforms.

My current academic path connects chemistry, chemistry education, and chemical and biological engineering, with a focus on detection strategies that can translate small physicochemical signals into clear analytical readouts.
