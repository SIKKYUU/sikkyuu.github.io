About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Chemical and Biological Engineering at Korea University. My research interests include ultrasensitive detection, quantum biosensing, and signal amplification.

My academic background spans chemistry, chemistry education, and chemical and biological engineering. I am interested in analytical systems that translate subtle biochemical or electrochemical signals into clear, practical readouts for biosensing applications.
